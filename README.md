Communicate provides an AI-powered customer support platform that answers queries instantly in the brand’s voice across all channels. It transforms help center articles, tickets, and product documentation into a conversational agent, offering grounded answers, seamless handoff to human agents, and robust data privacy controls. Teams benefit from higher resolution rates, reduced deflection, and flexible pricing with a $1 activation and credit‑based usage.

**Areas:** AI · Customer Support · Chatbot · SaaS · Automation

## How this profile is made

API Evangelist profiles a company's public API surface the way a developer would find it: the website, developer portal and documentation, the specifications it publishes, its public repositories, and its pricing, status and changelog pages. From that it derives the properties above — contracts split per API, workflows, schemas, collections, governance rules, plans and rate limits — and scores them against the published [Kin Score rubric](https://github.com/api-evangelist/kin-score).

The artifacts are published on [APIs.io](https://apis.io), not in this repository. This repository is the public record for communicate-so: the place to say something is wrong, missing or out of date, and to ask for a re-score or a removal.
